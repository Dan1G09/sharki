About Us

Sharkia is an electronics and office technology retailer, founded in 2026. We offer solutions that support both your workplace and your home: copying machines, computers, laptops, printers, projectors, phones and shredders from well-known brands. Our products are chosen for interoperability and flexibility, so they perform reliably wherever work happens, for individuals as well as for businesses, including companies abroad.
